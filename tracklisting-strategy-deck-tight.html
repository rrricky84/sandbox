The thesis

One canonical tracklist per show — assembled from every source, confidence-scored, spent three ways: listener experience, creator value, label reporting.

The core claim

Move any one of these alone and the listener feels nothing.

The plan

Three pillars, solved together

Supply

Make tracklists automatic

Ship the desktop app. Add Rekordbox. Autocomplete the editor. Instrument attach-rate.

Truth

One resolved tracklist

Creator × fingerprint × Trackdrop watermark, per row, with confidence. Creators confirm the machine; the machine enriches the creators.

Experience

Keep what you hear

Reveal as heard. Track history. Save the track, follow the artist, find more shows featuring them.

Watermarks cover fingerprinting's blind spot: unreleased promo material — detected deterministically, not probabilistically.
